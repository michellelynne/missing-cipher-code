# SKILL: Follow-Up Drafter

**From:** Ending a Conversation (and the Networking Worksheet's Exit and Follow Up)

## When to load this skill

Load this skill the day after an event. If the reader liked someone, they connected at the event and
saved any further conversation for the follow-up so nobody felt pressured. Real connections continue
after the event; this is the message that continues them.

## Required inputs

For each person:

1. **Name,** and how they connected (LinkedIn or another way).
2. **Where they met.**
3. **One thing they talked about,** ideally something specific the other person said.
4. **Whether the reader can meet in person** or only virtually.

## What this skill produces

A message per person that:

- **Is short.** Three or four sentences.
- **References the conversation,** so it could not have been sent to anyone else.
- **Invites them to coffee,** in person or virtually.
- **Makes declining effortless.** If they do not want to, it is easy for them to ignore. No deadline,
  no guilt, no "just following up" chain later.
- **Asks for nothing else.** No referral, no favor, no pitch. Connections grown over time help when you
  need them; asking at the first follow-up ends them.

Two variants per person: a slightly warmer one and a slightly more professional one, so the reader
can pick what matches the conversation.

## Prompt the reader can run directly

> I met [NAME] at [EVENT] yesterday and we connected on [LINKEDIN OR OTHER]. We talked about
> [SPECIFIC TOPIC OR SOMETHING THEY SAID].
>
> Draft a short follow-up, three or four sentences, that references our conversation and asks if
> they'd like to get a coffee, either in person or virtually. Make it low pressure and easy for them
> to ignore. Do not ask for a referral or any other favor. Give me two versions, one warmer and one
> more professional.

## What to tell the reader

- **Rewrite it in your voice.** People can tell a template.
- **Send them the next day,** while you both remember the conversation.
- **Don't follow up on the follow-up** if they do not answer. Say hello at the next event instead.
- **Only follow up with people you want to keep talking to.** Not every conversation needs one.

## What this skill does not do

It does not send messages or track replies. It drafts a note worth sending and leaves the sending to you.
