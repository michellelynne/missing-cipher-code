# Continuity guide: The Tuesday Bake

## Characters

**Nadia Haddad** — owner of Haddad's Bakery. 34 (birthday in March). Green eyes, dark curly hair she ties back with a blue bandana, left-handed. Took over the bakery from her late father two years before the book opens. Calls Marco "Marc" only when she is angry. Does not know (as of Chapter 7) that Marco has been offered a job at the hotel.

**Marco Ruiz** — head baker, 41. Gray beard, burn scar on his right forearm. Has worked at the bakery for eleven years. Received the hotel job offer in Chapter 6 and has told no one. Says "mija" to Nadia when he is being gentle.

**Mrs. Feld** — regular, 80s, buys one rye loaf every morning at seven.

## Timeline

- Chapter 6: Sunday, October 12. Marco gets the hotel offer.
- Chapter 7: Monday, October 13. The oven fails during the morning bake.

## Places

- Haddad's Bakery, corner of Fourth and Alder. The ovens are in the back; the front window faces east.

## Objects

- The bakery's only key to the walk-in cooler: Nadia keeps it on a cord around her neck.
- The hotel's offer letter: in Marco's coat pocket.
